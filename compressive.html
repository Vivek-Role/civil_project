1. Aim

To determine the compressive strength of fiber-reinforced M50 grade concrete across varying fiber volume fractions (Vf) at 7 days (early strength development) and 28 days (design acceptance) per IS 516 (Part 1 / Section 1), evaluating load-bearing capacity and cracking mechanics.

2. Theory & Governing Equations

Uniaxial compressive loading induces lateral tensile strain via Poisson's effect. In plain concrete, this causes sudden splitting or explosive conical shear failure. Discontinuous fibers provide three-dimensional lateral confinement, bridging opening micro-cracks and converting brittle failure into pseudo-ductile crushing with sustained residual load capacity.

Compressive strength: fc = P / A

fc = Compressive strength (MPa or N/mm²)
P = Maximum failure load (N)
A = Loaded cross-sectional area (mm²)
