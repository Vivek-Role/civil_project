1. Aim

To determine the compressive strength of fiber-reinforced M50 grade concrete across varying fiber volume fractions (Vf) at 7 days (early strength development) and 28 days (design acceptance) per IS 516 (Part 1 / Section 1), evaluating load-bearing capacity and cracking mechanics.

2. Theory & Governing Equations

Uniaxial compressive loading induces lateral tensile strain via Poisson's effect. In plain concrete, this causes sudden splitting or explosive conical shear failure. Discontinuous fibers provide three-dimensional lateral confinement, bridging opening micro-cracks and converting brittle failure into pseudo-ductile crushing with sustained residual load capacity.

Compressive strength: fc = P / A

fc = Compressive strength (MPa or N/mm²)
P = Maximum failure load (N)
A = Loaded cross-sectional area (mm²)

4. Apparatus Required

S. No. Apparatus Specification / Standards Purpose / Operational Details
1 Compression Testing Machine (CTM) Calibrated, conforming to IS 516 Uniaxial compressive load application at a uniform rate.
2 Cube Moulds Machined cast iron/steel (IS 10086) Casting specimens with rigid base plates and orthogonal interior faces.
3 Tamping Rod Diameter, length, bullet/rounded ends Manual compaction of concrete in layers (35 uniform strokes per layer).
4 Vibrating Table Conforming to IS 2505 / laboratory standard External mechanical vibration to compact concrete cubes and eliminate entrapped air voids.
5 Needle Vibrator (Internal Compactor) Needle diameter, high-frequency vibrator Internal consolidation to ensure thorough compaction around fibers and prevent fiber clustering.

7 Curing Tank Thermostatically controlled water bath Continuous moist submerged curing for 7-day and 28-day hydration.
